# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ChantierMobile is a private, proprietary project, but the same basic standard applies
to anyone with access to this repository, its issue tracker, or its communication
channels: treat collaborators with respect.

In practice:

- Give and receive feedback on code, not on people
- Assume good intent in reviews and discussions; ask before assuming
- Keep disagreements focused on the technical trade-off at hand
- Respect that contributors work across different languages (this is a French/English
  bilingual product) and time zones

Examples of unacceptable behavior: harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, personal
attacks, publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would reasonably
make a collaborator feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (the repository, pull requests, code review
comments, any linked issue tracker or chat) and in any other setting where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **dieudonneishara@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled privately and
as promptly as possible. Consequences for a violation are at the project owner's
discretion and may range from a private warning to loss of repository access.
